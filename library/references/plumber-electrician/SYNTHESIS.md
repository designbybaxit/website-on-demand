## Trade-specific service menus (from demos — copy only when client offers)

**Plumbing:** emergency repairs, residential/commercial, fixture install, water heater, gas line, hydronic, thaw frozen pipes, drain cleaning, leak detection, toilet repair, sewer/blocked drains, bathroom remodel plumbing.

**Electrical:** emergency, lighting, EV charger, switches, panel upgrade, wiring/rewiring, safety inspections, troubleshooting, circuit breaker, solar panel, generators, USB sockets, surge protection, ceiling fan, smart home.

**Handyman extras (GC profile only):** painting, carpentry, furniture assembly, AC maintenance, renovating.

---

## Trust & safety claims — require client evidence

Do **not** invent or keep demo defaults:

- Licensed / certified / insured / bonded
- Licence or certification numbers (e.g. VoltEdge “Licensed #184594”, NFPA 70E)
- “24/7” / “365 days” emergency (only if they said so)
- “No call-out fees”, senior discounts, weekend no-extra-charge
- “100% satisfaction guarantee”, warranty lengths (30 days, 7 days…)
- Award counts, branch counts, “50+ cities”
- Fake skill % or “350K+ clients”
- Membership plan prices

If missing → leave section out / placeholder list.

## Emergency & CTA patterns

- Persistent phone in header + floating/emergency button
- “Need Emergency Service? Call …” band with hours claim
- Dual CTA: **Call** (urgent) + **Get free estimate** (planned work)
- WhatsApp-first remains our product default; map Call → wa.me or tel: per client preference
- Quote form fields seen: name, phone, email, service type, address/city — keep minimal
