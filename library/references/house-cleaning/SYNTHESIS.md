## Residential vs office/commercial (source patterns)

| Aspect | Residential (source) | Commercial (source) |
|---|---|---|
| Services | Home, apartment, deep, move-in/out, carpet, windows, laundry, upholstery, disinfection | Office, mall, desks/electronics wipe, restrooms, breakroom, high-traffic floors |
| Pricing shape | **Per visit** tiers (Briva Basic/Standard/Deep) | Weekly/bi-weekly/custom by sq ft or size (Briva Office Starter / Growth / Custom); Bixol uses employee-count plans — **treat as bad filler**, not a model |
| Trust copy | Homeowner / tenant / move-out reviews | Office manager / property manager / business owner reviews |
| Scheduling | Book online, flexible lifestyle fit | Recurring weekly/multi-day, supervisor checks |

Seed should pick residential-only, commercial-only, or both from client services — don’t invent a commercial menu for a home-only maid service.

## Booking / quote structures (source)

- **ClanyEco:** Free Quote → Clean → Relax; “instant pricing” / free quote online; FAQ covers cost drivers (size, condition, services).
- **Briva:** Book Cleaning Online + Request Free Cleaning Quote; plan cards as conversion mid-page.
- **Bixol:** “Get a estimate” with service dropdown (House / Indoor / Plumbing / Bathroom).
- **Cleanza:** Schedule → Free Estimate → Team Arrives → Enjoy; FAQ on booking, insured, recurring, service area.
- **Clerex:** Request free quote → surface assessment → clean → final check (exterior specialty).
- **Goodust:** Book an Appointment band + phone/email.

Our product default remains WhatsApp / book with service-specific prefilled text; map quote forms to minimal fields (name, phone, service type, property type home/office, optional beds/baths or sq ft **only if client wants them**).

## Unsafe / unverified claims to avoid

- Insured & bonded / background-checked / vetted (unless client confirms)
- “100% satisfaction guarantee” / free re-clean (unless offered)
- Eco-friendly / water-quality guarantees (unless true)
- Fake stats: awards, “80k+ companies”, “countries globally”, skill % bars (Goodust)
- Invented Google rating / review counts
- “Available 7 days” / city coverage counts without client say-so
- Plumbing / roofing / house-fixing service cards from multipurpose themes
- HR-style employee-tier pricing (Bixol)
- Presenting Briva $99/$149/$199 as our default prices
