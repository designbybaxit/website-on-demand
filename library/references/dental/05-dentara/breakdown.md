## Fonts

Inclusive Sans 500 (headings, H1 ~70px) + Inter Tight (body).

## Buttons and corners

Pill (30px radius); dark-green solid, mint solid, or underlined text link with arrow.

## Motion and interaction

fadeInUp/fadeIn entrances, counters, image-comparison slider (TwentyTwenty), video popup, Owl/Swiper.

## What makes it feel premium (or not)

The best of the set. One accent colour used with discipline, the mint swash under one word per heading (same idea as our serif-italic accent word), photo-led cards with frosted labels, the staircase process, the bento reviews and a dedicated insurance/payment section that answers a real patient worry.
