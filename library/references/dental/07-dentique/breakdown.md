## Section order

1. **Hero card**: Header and hero sit inside one big rounded **cyan gradient card** with margin around it. Left: H1 with accent words **underlined by a yellow scribble** (animated), paragraph, yellow button + round play-video button, avatar stack with member count. Centre: cut-out dentist with a soft glow circle. Right: 3 stacked white stat cards (years, specialists, satisfaction %).
2. **Feature row**: 3 items with coloured square icons (teal/yellow) and short text.
3. **About split**: Photo with an overlapping teal badge card ("trusted by 100+"); H2, text, vision/mission mini-icons, yellow button + CEO avatar and name.
4. **Services**: 3 cards: photo on top, coloured square icon overlapping the photo bottom edge, title, text, "learn more".
5. **Testimonials**: Cyan panel: 2x2 frosted cards on the left, heading + button on the right.
6. **Skills split**: Heading + 3 progress bars left; photo with a floating "work hours" card and a "play video" card right.
7. **Steps**: 4 steps in a row with icons and big faint numbers 01–04; the hovered/second card lifts with a shadow.
8. **Appointment band**: Cyan gradient band: cut-out dentist left, white form card right (name, email, date, service, message).
9. **Blog**: 4 cards.
10. **Newsletter band**: Cyan rounded band with input + yellow button.
11. **Footer**: White, 4 columns, rounded teal copyright bar.

## Palette (from computed styles / theme colour variables)

- `#8EEBFA` light cyan (hero gradient)
- `#179DC2` teal blue (primary)
- `#07445C` deep teal (headings)
- `#FFD100` yellow (CTA, scribble)
- `#E4FAFF` ice bg
- `#5A7594` body

## Fonts

Plus Jakarta Sans 600 (headings) + Poppins (body).

## Buttons and corners

8px radius, uppercase, letter-spaced; yellow with deep-teal text, or teal with white.

## Motion and interaction

Animated text (scribble underline), fun-fact counters, hover-from-left button fills, video popup buttons.

## What makes it feel premium (or not)

The framed hero card, the hand-drawn scribble accent, cut-out + glow + floating stat cards. Busy but energetic; works for a bright, friendly clinic.
