## Section order

1. **Top bar + header**: Green phone tab + email/address + socials; blue header bar with green pill "book an appointment".
2. **Hero**: Blue-to-green gradient overlay over a clinic photo; text left; cut-out dentist holding a teeth model right.
3. **3-panel strip (overlaps hero)**: Emergency phone (navy), opening hours with clock icons (blue), and a **quick booking form** (green: name, phone, email, date, submit).
4. **Trust + counters**: Centred heading over a dotted world map, 3 counters with line icons.
5. **Services**: 3x3 cards: green square icon, title, text, green pill.
6. **Emergency banner**: Photo of a person with toothache, frosted card with headline + button.
7. **Why choose us**: Heading, text, button and photo left; 2x2 feature cards right (one card filled blue).
8. **Doctor intro**: Text + signature left, large cut-out doctor right.
9. **Testimonials**: Carousel of cards over big concentric circles.
10. **Skills + video**: Progress bars with bubble percentages; dentist photo with play button.
11. **FAQ**: Full-width accordion with ? icons.
12. **CTA band**: "Need help?" with cut-out dentist and button.
13. **Footer**: Blue, 4 columns.

## Fonts

Poppins 600 (headings) + Heebo (body).

## Buttons and corners

Pill (30px), green solid, small arrow.

## Motion and interaction

Lots of Elementor entrances (fadeIn L/R/Up/Down, zoomIn, bounceIn), animated numbers, Swiper testimonials, hover animator.

## What makes it feel premium (or not)

Practical rather than premium: the 3-panel emergency/hours/booking strip is genuinely useful. Visually dated (stock cut-outs, heavy gradients, too many animation types).
