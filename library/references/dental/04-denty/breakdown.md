4. **About split**: H2 + sub-line, **numbered list** (01/02/03) separated by hairlines, paragraph, blue pill; tall portrait of the dentist right.
5. **Why patients trust us**: 4 airy icon cards.
6. **Team**: 3 cards with a thick amber frame and amber caption panel (name, speciality, small pill).
7. **Full-bleed CTA**: Huge smiling close-up photo with a dark glass card bottom-left (headline, line, button).
8. **Testimonials**: 3 cards each tinted a different pastel gradient (peach, mint, grey), large quote text, avatar + role.
9. **FAQ**: Full-width accordion with solid green bars, first item open.
10. **Blog**: 3 cards with category chips.
11. **Contact**: Form card on soft grey gradient (name, email, phone, subject, message) + photo + green info card (address, email, phone with circle icons).
12. **Footer**: Dark slate: big "ready for a healthier smile" headline + pill, small menu/office columns.

## Buttons and corners

Pill (48px radius), Inter 600, capitalised; solid blue, white, or ghost.

## Motion and interaction

fadeIn / slideInUp / slideInDown entrances; "hover-from-arise" gradient fill on cards; animated circle progress.

## What makes it feel premium (or not)

The bento hero and the glass stat chips on the photo, pastel-tinted cards that vary per card, and generous whitespace. Modern 2025 SaaS-style feel.
