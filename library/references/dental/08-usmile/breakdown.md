## Palette (from computed styles / theme colour variables)

- `#00CAE0` cyan (accent)
- `#000000` headings
- `#DBEEFA` ice blue
- `#F3FAFF` lightest blue
- `#444444` body

## Fonts

DM Sans only (500 headings, regular body).

## Buttons and corners

Full pill (100px radius), uppercase small, cyan or white or black.

## Motion and interaction

Custom scroll reveal + reveal animation (anime.js + scrollMonitor), counters, TwentyTwenty compare slider, video popup.

## What makes it feel premium (or not)

Consistent **arch** shape language (hero, FAQ, team) and very calm ice-blue + black palette; the arc testimonials are unusual. Single font keeps it clean.
