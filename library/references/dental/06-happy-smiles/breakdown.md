- `#F3FFFB` lightest mint
- `#546E7A` body

## Fonts

Marcellus (serif display, 400) + Nunito (body). Soft and friendly.

## Buttons and corners

Small rectangular buttons with slight rounding, uppercase tiny text; yellow or periwinkle fills.

## Motion and interaction

Custom pop-in / fade-in-right / fade-in-up, "jello" wobble on badges, vanilla-tilt on cards, basicScroll parallax, anime.js, counters, before/after widget.

## What makes it feel premium (or not)

The organic blob hero, the rotating text badge, scooped-corner video frame and the pastel trio (mint, butter, periwinkle) with a serif display. Best reference for a kids/family dental kit.
