# 03-timan: Timan – Dental Clinic WordPress theme (WPBakery + Slider Revolution)

- URL: https://demo.casethemes.net/timan/?storefront=envato-elements (loaded OK)
- Page length: 8,923 px
- Files: `desktop-full.png` (1440 wide), `mobile-full.png` (390 wide @2x), `hero-desktop.png`, `hero-mobile.png`, `extract.json` (computed styles, fonts, widgets, section list)

## Section order

1. **Header**: White; logo, nav, cart/search icons, phone pill, and a solid plum "get appointment" block flush to the right edge.
2. **Hero**: Full-width photo **slider** (Slider Revolution) with dark overlay, centred eyebrow + big H1, coral solid + white outline buttons, round scroll-down button.
3. **Info bar (attached under hero)**: 3 coloured tiles in a row: call us (plum), opening hours (coral), make an appointment (plum), each with a line icon.
4. **Service cards**: 4 bordered cards with line tooth icons.
5. **Appointment + welcome**: Lavender section: a white **appointment form card** with plum header (name, phone, date, time, consent) left; photo + welcome text right.
6. **Gallery**: "Photo showcase" masonry (isotope) with a huge faint **ghost word** behind the section title.
7. **Emergency banner**: Plum gradient over a big smiling close-up, 24h icon, phone number, "need emergency help" headline.
8. **Services**: 6 cards with soft pastel gradient icon blobs; ghost word behind title again.
9. **Pricing**: 3 plans on plum; middle plan outlined in coral with a coral round arrow button.
10. **Blog**: 3 post cards.
11. **Stats + quote**: Plum band: 4 counters with icons on the left, a testimonial speech bubble on the right.
12. **Footer**: 4 columns incl. a photo-gallery thumbnail grid; coral short rules above headings.

## Palette (from computed styles / theme colour variables)

- `#614379` plum (primary)
- `#FF8A8B` coral (CTA)
- `#070A22` near-black headings
- `#8F8BB8` muted lavender text
- `#696FA2` secondary text
- `#F4F7F7` light bg

## Fonts

Poppins 600 (headings) + Rubik (body/labels).

## Buttons and corners

Square, uppercase, letter-spaced; coral solid + white ghost outline on dark.

## Motion and interaction

Slider Revolution parallax hero, counters, Owl carousel, Isotope filter gallery, Magnific lightbox, WPBakery "animate when visible".

## What makes it feel premium (or not)

The attached 3-tile info bar under the hero (contact, hours, booking at a glance) and the giant ghost words behind section titles. Otherwise an older 2018-style theme; pricing/blog are generic.
