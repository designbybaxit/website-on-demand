## Section order

1. **Header**: White bar: logo left, uppercase small nav, violet square "phone number" button right.
2. **Hero**: Full-bleed teal colour field (no photo background). Left: tiny letter-spaced eyebrow, a huge heavy two-word H1 (~85px, weight 900) and an uppercase sub-line, one square violet CTA. Right: cut-out photo of a dentist with a patient standing inside a tall pale **arch** shape, with a plant for depth.
3. **Services panel (overlaps hero)**: White panel pulled up over the hero bottom: 8 icon tiles (4x2) with thin dividers, plus a photo tile on the right with a rotated vertical "about clinic" link.
4. **Intro split**: Eyebrow + H2 left, paragraph + underlined "read more" text link right.
5. **Photo mosaic**: 3-column collage of 4 clinic photos (one tall, two stacked, one tall) with a floating teal "8+ years" badge on the bottom-left corner.
6. **Lead dentist feature**: Two staggered photos (equipment + portrait) left; dentist name as eyebrow, H2, text, text link right.
7. **Consultation CTA band**: Mint gradient band: cut-out family + dentist photo left, big uppercase headline right, row of round contact-channel icons (Messenger, Instagram, phone, WhatsApp).
8. **Team**: 5 small cards: circular avatar with a green "verified" tick, name, role, small "follow on" + star "rating" footer split.
9. **Reviews**: Left: title panel with a "5-star reviews on Google" dark badge. Right: swiper carousel of white review cards with stars, quote marks and avatar.
10. **Footer**: Navy, logo + tagline + socials, 4 link columns, thin copyright bar.

## Palette (from computed styles / theme colour variables)

- `#482AFD` electric violet (buttons)
- `#C6FFF1` mint tint
- `#E9EFF8` pale blue section bg
- `#52CD81` green (ticks)
- `#5A5A5A` body text

## Fonts

Red Hat Display (display, 800-900 for H1/H2, uppercase small labels) + Poppins (body, card titles). Both Google Fonts.

## Buttons and corners

Square corners (0 radius), uppercase, letter-spaced, solid violet; secondary actions are underlined text links.

## Motion and interaction

Elementor fadeInUp entrance on hero and text blocks; Swiper review carousel; Magnific lightbox. Little else.

## What makes it feel premium (or not)

The scale contrast (giant heavy H1 next to tiny letter-spaced labels), the arch-framed cut-out photo on a flat colour field, and the services panel overlapping the hero edge. Weak spots: lorem copy, identical team cards, empty space under reviews.
