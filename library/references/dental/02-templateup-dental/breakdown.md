## Section order

1. **Top bar**: Indigo strip: address, email, phone left; social icons right.
2. **Header**: Transparent over hero; pink pill "schedule a visit" CTA.
3. **Hero**: Indigo-tinted full-bleed clinic photo; left text (pink eyebrow, 3-line H1, short line, white outline pill); right a cut-out smiling woman in front of a big indigo circle with animated line-art rings (Lottie).
4. **Treatments strip (overlaps hero)**: Pink intro tile ("get to know some treatments") + 3 white icon cards in one row.
5. **Why split**: Patient photo left; eyebrow, H2, two paragraphs and two thin **progress bars** (pink/indigo) right.
6. **Promo tiles**: Two side-by-side cards, each = coloured text panel + photo (indigo/whitening, light/veneers).
7. **Pricing**: 3 monthly plans; middle plan is a taller dark-indigo card that rises above the others; tick/cross feature lists; pill buttons.
8. **Treatment promo band**: Split indigo/pink card with a cut-out doctor in the middle and two small quote boxes on the right.
9. **Aligners split + hours**: Text left; photo right with an **opening hours card** floating over it (days and times joined by dotted leaders).
10. **Technique split**: Microscope photo left, text + button right, Lottie circles as decoration.
11. **CTA band**: Indigo overlay on a corridor photo, centred headline, pink pill.
12. **Footer**: Logo + blurb + round pink social icons, link list, contact list, and a pink **newsletter card**.

## Palette (from computed styles / theme colour variables)

- `#3A3089` indigo (primary)
- `#30266D` deep indigo
- `#F9669D` candy pink (CTA)
- `#F5F5F5` light grey bg
- `#272A2A` headings
- `#7A7A7A` body

## Fonts

Yusei Magic (rounded, slightly hand-drawn display) + Roboto Flex (body). Playful, not clinical.

## Buttons and corners

Full pill (100px radius), uppercase; pink solid or white outline.

## Motion and interaction

Elementor zoomIn/fadeIn/fadeInLeft/rotateInUpRight entrances, Lottie line animations around the hero model, a parallax text block.

## What makes it feel premium (or not)

Strong two-colour identity (indigo + pink) applied consistently; overlap of the treatments strip on the hero; hours card floating on a photo. Feels more "beauty clinic" than medical.
